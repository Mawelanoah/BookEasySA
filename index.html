Frequently asked questions

How do customers request an appointment?

Open a business booking page, choose a service, pick a date and available time, then submit your name and phone or WhatsApp number. No customer account is required in this version.

Are appointments confirmed immediately?

No. Requests start as pending. The business owner must confirm the appointment.

How do businesses register?

Use Register Your Business. Real account creation requires a secure backend (next phase).

How do owners manage services?

From the demo dashboard, open Services to add, edit, or remove services and prices. Demo changes stay in your browser only.

How can I contact support?

Use the support form on this page. A live delivery system is not connected yet.
