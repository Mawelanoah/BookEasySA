FAQ & Support

Straight answers about the current demonstration.

What is BookEasy SA?

A booking and profile platform for South African appointment-based businesses. This public site is still a frontend demonstration.

Who can use it?

Nail technicians, hair salons, lash and brow artists, spas, barbers, and similar businesses — plus their customers.

How does a customer request an appointment?

Open a business booking page, choose a service, pick a date and time, enter name and phone, then submit a request.

When is a booking considered confirmed?

Only after the business owner accepts it. A submitted request starts as pending, not confirmed.

Is BookEasy SA free during testing?

Yes. Early access is free during testing. Future paid plans will be announced before any charges apply.

Can a business change services and prices?

Yes — in the demo dashboard you can edit services in this browser. Permanent changes need the database stage.

How can a business express interest in joining?

Use the support form below or the registration prototype. Messages are not delivered until a backend is connected.

How will customer information be protected?

See our draft Privacy Policy. Real accounts will follow POPIA-minded practices; the demo mostly keeps data in your browser.
