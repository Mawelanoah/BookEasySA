How customers book

No app download. Open a link, pick a service, request a time.

1

Open the booking page

Customer taps the business link from WhatsApp, Instagram, or Facebook.

2

Choose service, date & time

See prices and durations, then pick an available slot that suits them.

3

Send a request

They leave their name and WhatsApp number. The owner confirms from the dashboard.

For business owners

Present your services online, share one link, and manage requests in one place.

1️⃣

Create your business page

Add your name, logo, description, services, prices, photos, and opening hours. No coding required.

2️⃣

Get your booking link

Each business will get a unique public URL (example shape: bookeasy.co.za/your-business). Demo only for now.

3️⃣

Share your link

Post it on WhatsApp, Status, Instagram, Facebook, or your existing website so customers can book anytime.

4️⃣

Manage appointments

Review pending requests, confirm or cancel, and keep services up to date from your dashboard.
