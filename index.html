How it works

A simple flow for both the business and their customers.

1

Business sets up profile

Add services, prices, hours and photos. Share a unique booking link on WhatsApp or social media.

2

Customer books online

Opens the link, chooses a service, date and time, then submits contact details — no app required.

3

Owner manages requests

View, confirm or reschedule appointments from a private dashboard. Track basic income later.
