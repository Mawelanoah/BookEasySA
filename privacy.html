Privacy Policy

Draft for review before launch. Last updated: October 2026.

BookEasy SA is being developed as an online booking platform for small appointment-based businesses in South Africa.

What we may collect later

When secure accounts and bookings are connected, we may process business profile details, service information, and customer booking details such as name, phone number, preferred appointment time, and optional notes.

Current demonstration

This public demo does not send personal data to a BookEasy SA server. Some demo actions store information only in your browser (localStorage). Clearing browser data removes it.

How we will use data

Customer details provided for a booking request should only be used to manage that appointment. Business owners remain responsible for how they handle customer information outside the platform.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Future hosting or messaging providers will be disclosed when connected.

Your rights

Under South African POPIA principles, individuals should be able to request access to or correction of their personal information once real accounts are live. Contact details for privacy requests will be published when a support channel is available.

Changes

This draft will be updated before collecting real user data at scale. Continued use of the live product after changes will be explained on this page.

This page is informational and is not legal advice.
